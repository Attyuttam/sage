Your Day 1 mental model

You should now have this:

More log data
      ↓
More characters
      ↓
Generally more tokens
      ↓
More context consumed
      ↓
Potentially more computation/latency

BUT

More relevant context
      ↓
More evidence available
      ↓
Potentially better reasoning



And therefore:

           TOO LITTLE
               ↓
        insufficient evidence
               ↓
          poor answer

             SWEET SPOT
               ↓
       enough relevant context
               ↓
          good answer

           TOO MUCH
               ↓
     unnecessary context
               ↓
      latency / cost / noise

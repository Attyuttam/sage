1. What is a token?
- A token is a chunk that the LLM understands. It can be a single word but 
not necessarily, its a piece from the context that the LLM uses to infer
answers.

2. What is a context window?
- Context window is the maximum number of tokens that an LLM can process at once. 

3. Why can't we say 7,530 characters = 7,530 tokens?
- Because the tokens can be half of the word or something. It need not be the 
whole word itself. Thus there can be more or less number of tokens compared 
to the number of words

4. Looking at your experiment, why did the 5-entry analysis likely differ from 
the 15-entry analysis?
- Because the 5 entry analysis did not have all the information that the 
15 entry analysis had. Thus it could not determine the root cause of the incident.

5. Does more context always mean a better answer? Why?
- Not necessarily, because sometimes too much context would also mean
a lot of unnecessary information that would cause more latency or sometimes irrelevant 
results.

6. Why might the 65-entry experiment be less attractive than the 
30-entry experiment even though both produced essentially the same analysis?
- Latency was higher and the result was similar.

7. What are we actually measuring with Prompt character count in today's experiment?
- we are trying to understand whether more tokens means much better answer
or there's a threshold to the quality of the answer that we get.

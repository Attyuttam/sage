              LLM Application
                    │
        ┌───────────┼───────────┐
        ↓           ↓           ↓
     Quality     Latency    Throughput
                    │
              ┌─────┴─────┐
              ↓           ↓
             P50        P95/P99


So production systems usually care about percentiles:

P50 → median
P95 → 95% of requests are faster than this
P99 → 99% are faster than this

For example:

P50 = 20s
P95 = 35s
P99 = 90s

This tells you much more than:

Average = 24s

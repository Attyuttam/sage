Why did Zero-shot fail even though the logs were explicit?
- the value of severity was not explicitly defined and thus the LLM considered the severity to the best of its understanding.
But unfortunately that was not in the permissible set of values.

Why did Explicit prompting perform better?
- Because the LLm knew what kind of values are expected out of it by the user.

Why isn't Few-shot automatically better than Explicit prompting?
- Because bad examples might end up making the LLM produce bad results.

What is the difference between valid JSON, valid schema, and correct analysis?
- valid JSON means that the JSON is well-formed.
- valid schema means that the JSON is valid and also follows the schema.
- correct analysis means that the JSON is valid and also follows the schema and is also correct in terms of the input logs.
